---
name: legal-and-licenses
description: Never gate engineering architecture, upstream absorption, or game assets on licenses or legal speculation
type: feedback
---

- Licenses never decide or restrict architecture, linking, vendoring, or fork absorption (`[patch]`, Blink/CEF, WebKit, GPL/MPL/MIT crates). Absorb, flatten, and modernize upstream source directly.
- Never characterize proprietary assets, add unrequested copyright/trademark disclaimers, or exclude/gate assets (such as game fonts, sprites, or logos) on legal grounds. Execute the technical work only.
